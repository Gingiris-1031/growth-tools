Key takeaways

Use this skill

What to provide

Product URL, decision to make, target market, competitor links and time window. Unknown historical data is allowed.

What you get

A source table, comparable-stage analysis, alternative explanations and a next-action card with an owner and review condition.

Starter prompt

Compare our tool workspace with this competitor landing page. Explain which comparisons are valid, then propose one change and a validation plan.

Example decision

Illustrative example: Observed: a competitor has 17 marketing screens; our workspace has 2. Decision: compare visitor tasks and onboarding friction, not page length. Test one clearer first-action label.

Avoid these shortcuts

  • Do not turn a teaching example into a universal rule.
  • Do not substitute views, intent or plans for observed business outcomes.
  • Do not publish or contact people merely because a draft exists.

FAQ

Can I use this without Similarweb or a paid research API?

Yes. Start from supplied pages and visible evidence. Mark unavailable traffic or historical data explicitly; do not infer a growth channel from a Direct traffic share.
