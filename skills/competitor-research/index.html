Starter prompt

Use competitor-research-playbook. Compare our launch-stage SaaS with one relevant competitor’s earliest available content. Separate observed facts from inference and propose a test we can run this week.

请使用 competitor-research-playbook。对照一个相关竞品最早可得的内容，研究我们发布阶段的 SaaS；区分事实与推断，提出本周能执行的测试。
