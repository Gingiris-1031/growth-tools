SEO &amp; GEO Dual-Engine Playbook

Rank on Google AND get cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini — with one content system.

Key takeaways

  • Start from BOFU — do high-intent terms (pricing, comparisons) first; they convert highest, then work up to educational content.
  • Authentic voice IS the best E-E-A-T — don't let AI write like AI; founder voice + real numbers + first-hand stories.

What this is

Google ranking and AI-search citation are two different games with one content system. SEO rewards backlinks + keywords; GEO rewards structured data + E-E-A-T authenticity. This playbook runs both engines from the same content — so a single page earns Google rankings and shows up inside ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude answers.

⚠️ Product type note: This playbook is optimized for dev tools / OSS / B2B SaaS. If your product is a 2C consumer app, education platform, or game, key parameters differ — lower keyword volume threshold (50 vs 300), YMYL E-E-A-T requirements, and different cold-start channels (Reddit communities / 小红书 / TikTok / Quora instead of PH/GitHub/HN). The 5 core principles of SEO + GEO

  1. 1

    1 · Start from BOFU, work upward

    Build the highest-intent, highest-converting keywords first (pricing, comparisons, alternatives), then expand up the funnel to educational content. Revenue-adjacent pages compound fastest.

  2. 2

    2 · Authentic voice is the best E-E-A-T

    Don't let AI write like AI. Use the founder's voice, real numbers, and first-hand stories — authenticity signals are what both Google E-E-A-T and AI engines reward.

  3. 3

    3 · Structured = citable

    Add Key-Stats tables, FAQ schema, and comparison matrices to every page. Structured, answer-first content is exactly what AI engines extract and cite.

  4. 4

    4 · IndexNow is GEO infrastructure

    Wire up IndexNow so every content update pings Bing and AI search in seconds. Don't wait weeks for organic crawl when you've shipped something citable.

  5. 5

    5 · Comparison pages are SEO gold mines

    Build one dedicated page per competitor ("X vs Y", "X alternatives") to capture decision-stage users — these are the highest-ROI pages for both SEO and GEO.

Anti-patterns (what kills SEO + GEO)

  • AI-sounding generic copy — no E-E-A-T signal, no citation.
  • Cherry-picked comparison rows — erodes trust and costs you AI citations.
  • Starting from TOFU educational content — slow, low-converting; do BOFU first.
  • Shipping content with no schema — invisible to AI extraction.

FAQ

What is GEO and how is it different from SEO?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is optimizing to be cited inside AI-search answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini), where the signals are structured data + E-E-A-T authenticity. SEO targets Google/Bing rankings via backlinks + keywords. This playbook wins both from one content set.

What's the single highest-ROI thing I can do?

Build comparison pages — one dedicated "X vs Y" or "X alternatives" page per competitor. They capture decision-stage users and are equally strong for Google ranking and AI citation.

Do I really need IndexNow?

For GEO, yes — it's the infrastructure that pushes updates to Bing and AI search in seconds instead of waiting on crawl cycles. It's a one-time setup that compounds on every publish.
