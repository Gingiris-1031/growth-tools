    Distribution, conversion, retention, or positioning — the bottleneck, not the symptom, points to the playbook.

Anti-patterns (growth mistakes this prevents)

  • Running an SLG playbook on a PLG product (or vice versa).
  • Marketing hard before you have PMF.
  • Chasing stars/launches when the real bottleneck is conversion.
  • Picking a channel because it's trendy, not because it fits your stage.

FAQ

How do I know which growth playbook I need?

Diagnose three things — product type (OSS / B2B SaaS / app / content), stage (pre-PMF / traction / scaling), and your actual bottleneck (distribution / conversion / retention / positioning). The routing table maps that to a specific playbook to install.

I'm pre-PMF — should I start marketing?

No. Before PMF, the highest-leverage move is validating the must-have through user interviews, not running acquisition. Growth Finder routes pre-PMF products to the User Interview playbook first.
