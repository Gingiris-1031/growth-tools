Use this skill

What to provide

Original writing or speech, context, author edits, disliked examples, desired language and audience.

What you get

A source inventory, provisional or confirmed voice guide, a test draft and a feedback log.

Starter prompt

Build a provisional voice guide from these originals. Separate my preferences from engagement patterns and test it on a held-out sample.

Example decision

Illustrative example: Observed: Chinese news posts outperform English tutorials. Decision: topic and language changed together, so neither proves a language preference. Ask for author feedback and test comparable material.

Avoid these shortcuts

  • Do not turn a teaching example into a universal rule.
  • Do not substitute views, intent or plans for observed business outcomes.
  • Do not publish or contact people merely because a draft exists.

FAQ

Do high-engagement posts define my authentic voice?

No. Engagement and author preference are different evidence. Keep counterexamples and test the guide on material not used to construct it.
