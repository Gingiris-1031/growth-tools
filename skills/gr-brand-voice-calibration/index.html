Key takeaways

  • Authors whose AI drafts sound generic or lose their voice when adapted between Chinese and English.

  • Receive: Source index; contextual voice rules; counterexamples; test draft; unresolved preferences

What this is

Build a reusable voice guide from original writing, speech and the author’s own corrections. 以本人原文、原话和亲手修改为依据，提炼能复用、可试写验证的文风指南。

Engagement is not proof of authentic voice. Private source archives are not published with the guide.

Use gr-brand-voice-calibration. Compare these original posts with my edits. Build a provisional voice guide and test it on one English post without inventing personal experience.
