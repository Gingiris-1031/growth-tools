npx skills add Gingiris-1031/solopreneur-coach-skill --skill gingiris-solopreneur-coach

Who is this for?

Aspiring solopreneurs with experience, an idea, or an interested client who need a manageable next step. Professional services are the deepest supported path; product and content ideas can use the starting and demand-validation stages.

Start with one message

Use gingiris-solopreneur-coach. I want to try building a one-person business but don't know where to start. Ask one easy question at a time and help me prepare one small action.

The coach helps define one uncertainty, prepares a usable draft, and suggests what real-world evidence to bring back. You can skip a question, correct an assumption, or ask for a direct draft.

Your path

  1. Find a starting point that fits your time and experience.
  2. Define a bounded offer or small experiment.
  3. Understand a real customer's task.
  4. Clarify scope and prepare a quote.
  5. Deliver, obtain acceptance, settle agreed payments, and review.

You can enter at any stage. A free sample, a compliment, a deposit and a completed paid job are recorded separately.

What does it need?

A compatible AI agent capable of loading the skill and its references. Model or host fees are separate. Core coaching needs no private document access or additional skills. Cross-session memory and proactive reminders are not guaranteed.

从能走的一小步开始

适合有经验却不知道如何服务化、有想法但时间有限，或已经有人询价的人。通过温和设问理清当前问题，当场做出有用草稿，再带着实际反馈调整。专业服务是目前最完整的路径，也支持产品与内容方向的起点和需求验证。

第一句话怎么说

请使用 gingiris-solopreneur-coach。我想尝试一人业务，但不知道从哪里开始。请一次问我一个容易回答的问题，帮我准备一个真正能做的小动作。

首次交流应得到暂定问题、小动作和邀约或服务说明等可用材料。可以跳过问题、纠正假设，也可以直接要求起草。

如何继续

回来说明上次计划、实际发生的事、匿名反馈和当前时间。没有行动也可以继续；下一步可以缩小、澄清、等待或暂停。

学习闭环是“行动—外部反馈—更新判断”；完整付费闭环还需确认范围、交付、验收和结清约定款项。免费试做、称赞和首款到账分别记录。

规则和五份阶段 SOP 均为中英文。默认跟随用户语言，不要求辞职或注册公司。不保证跨会话记忆或主动提醒；模型平台费用另计。
